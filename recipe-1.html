Spaghetti Bolognese

Ingredients

  • 500g spaghetti
  • 400g minced beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 can chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Olive oil

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan and fry the onion and garlic until soft.
  2. Add the minced beef and cook until browned.
  3. Stir in the tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  4. Simmer for 20–30 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti according to package instructions.
  6. Drain the spaghetti and serve with the sauce on top.

Prep time: 10 minutes

Cooking time: 30 minutes

Serves: 4 people
