Chicken Curry

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken breast, diced
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 can chopped tomatoes
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Olive oil

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan and fry the onion and garlic until soft.
  2. Add the chicken and cook until browned on all sides.
  3. Add the curry powder, cumin, and coriander – cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Stir in the chopped tomatoes and coconut milk.
  5. Simmer for 20–25 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  7. Serve with rice or naan bread.

Prep time: 15 minutes

Cooking time: 30 minutes

Serves: 4 people
